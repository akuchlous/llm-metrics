We are building an open-source benchmark that measures how large language model APIs perform in practice: how long they take to start answering, how long a full answer takes, how often requests fail and what they cost.

The site has two kinds of pages. Question pages explain how something is measured and what it means. Metric pages show the validated measurements themselves, with their sample size, time window and limitations.

Today we measure one model, OpenAI gpt-6-luna, with one fixed workload from one Google Cloud region. We do not publish rankings or comparisons between providers.
