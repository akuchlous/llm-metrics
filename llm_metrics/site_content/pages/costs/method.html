• What counts: every paid service used for this project: LLM API providers, Google Cloud (Cloud Run, Cloud Scheduler, Cloud Storage, network), website hosting, the domain and any other project-specific tools. Unrelated personal spending and our own time are not included.
  • Charges, not estimates: only amounts actually charged count toward a total. Invoices that are not yet paid are shown separately. Usage estimates, such as the benchmark's token-price cost, are never added, because the bill already includes that usage.
  • Unknown is not zero: a service with no reviewed bill is listed as unknown.
  • Currencies: amounts stay in the currency they were charged in. We do not convert currencies.
  • Privacy: invoice numbers, account numbers and payment details are never published. Each bill is described in words and identified by the first characters of its file's SHA-256 hash.
