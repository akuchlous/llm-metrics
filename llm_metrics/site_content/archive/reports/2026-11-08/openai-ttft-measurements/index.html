What are the measured OpenAI time to first token (TTFT) results?

Archived measurement snapshot, published 8 November 2026 snapshot. It is never changed after publication. See the current measurements.

What this page measures

This page shows client-observed time to first token for OpenAI gpt-6-luna: the time from just before a streaming request is made until the first chunk of answer text arrives. Every value comes from a validated, published dataset, and the sample size and data window are shown with it.

The numbers come from one fixed workload sent from one Google Cloud region. They are not a ranking and do not describe other prompts, models or locations.
